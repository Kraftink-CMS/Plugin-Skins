Once members link a verified Minecraft Java account, they can upload textures and preview them before saving.

- Compatible launchers can read and upload player textures through the API.
- Enable public PNG links when you want members to share new uploads.
- Apply skins in game with KraftLink and SkinsRestorer on a compatible Bukkit server. Cape files can be used on the site and by compatible launchers.

Open **Extensions → Skins and capes** to choose who can upload textures and which connected servers can apply them.
